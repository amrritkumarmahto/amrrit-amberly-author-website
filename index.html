She was hired to work inside the house.

She wasn't supposed to investigate it.

Mara Vale expects six months of quiet administrative work at Vellamere House, an isolated estate overlooking Kettle Lake. The salary is generous. The work is straightforward. And the Voss family seems determined to keep their distance.

Then Mara discovers a room nobody is supposed to enter.

The family says Elena Voss disappeared twelve years ago.

But a photograph places Elena alive six months after she was reported missing.

Mara begins pulling at the loose threads: unexplained footsteps, household records that don't add up, photographs that reveal details the family has not explained, and a family history that changes depending on who tells it.

Then she finds something stranger.

Years before Elena disappeared, she had been investigating an unnamed child connected to a former Vellamere employee named Clara Bell. At Larch Hill, another Voss property, Mara discovers Elena's handwritten research notes, references to the child, and two initials that may connect the old mystery to something much closer to home.

Then Mara learns the truth about her own arrival.

Victor Voss knew her name before the employment agency formally presented her as a candidate.

He also knows details about Mara's childhood that she never remembers giving the family.

Mara came to Vellamere believing she was an outsider investigating a family's secrets.

Now she has to consider a far more disturbing possibility:

What if she was brought there because she was already part of the story?

And if Elena spent years investigating the same mystery Mara has now uncovered, what did Elena discover before she disappeared?

The house has secrets.

The family has names.

And Mara has just discovered that her own story may be connected to theirs.

The Bed Was Still Warm is a psychological thriller about buried family secrets, missing years, concealed identities, and the dangerous consequences of discovering that your own past may not be as certain as you believed.

About the Author

Amrrit Amberly

Amrrit Amberly is a self-published author with a bachelor’s degree in English Literature. He writes psychological thrillers centered on family secrets, disappearances, hidden identities, deception, fractured relationships, and the disturbing truths people try hardest to hide.

Fascinated by the darker corners of human behavior, Amrrit’s stories explore what happens when ordinary lives begin to unravel and the people closest to us become impossible to trust.

With countless stories and worlds living in his head, Amrrit had always dreamed of becoming an author. After graduating in 2023, he began writing seriously, though for years he kept his stories to himself and never showed them to anyone.

In the autumn of 2026, he finally followed that dream and became a full-time author.

Amrrit is the author of The Bed Was Still Warm, the first book in A Reckoning of Names, a ten-book psychological thriller series.
