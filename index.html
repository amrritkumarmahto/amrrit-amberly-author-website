About the Author

Amrrit Amberly

Amrrit Amberly writes psychological thrillers that live in the quiet places — the familiar room, the trusted face, the name you thought you knew. Their fiction explores identity, memory, and the secrets people carry long after they should have let them go.

The Bed Was Still Warm is Amrrit Amberly’s debut novel and the first book in the A Reckoning of Names series.
